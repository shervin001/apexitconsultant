Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Apex IT Consultant ("we", "us") is a boutique SAP consultancy based in the Netherlands. This policy explains how we handle personal data from visitors of apexitconsultant.com and from people who apply to work with us.

What we collect

  • Application data — name, email address, and anything you choose to share with us: phone number, LinkedIn URL, role of interest, message, and CV.
  • Direct contact — information you send us by email, phone, or LinkedIn.

The website itself does not use tracking cookies or analytics that identify you.

Why we process it

We process application data solely to evaluate your application, contact you about it, and — if we both decide to continue — prepare an offer. The legal basis is taking steps at your request prior to entering into a contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in recruiting (art. 6(1)(f)).

How long we keep it

We keep application data for up to four weeks after the conclusion of the application process, unless you give us consent to keep it on file longer (up to one year) for future opportunities.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except processors we use to run our email and document infrastructure, under data processing agreements.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to restrict or object to its processing. Contact us at careers@apexitconsultant.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
