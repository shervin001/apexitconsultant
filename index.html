About Us

Deep technical craftsmanship, modern cloud architecture

Apex IT Consultant is a boutique SAP consultancy. We stay deliberately small so we can stay deliberately good: every consultant is a hands-on engineer who ships production code for some of Europe's most demanding enterprises.

We combine clean-core ABAP discipline with cloud-native thinking on the Business Technology Platform. Our people work with autonomy, own their architecture decisions, and are trusted by clients to lead — not just to staff.

If you care about the craft of SAP engineering, you'll feel at home here.

Engineering culture
